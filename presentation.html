Core Decision Engine

Transparent Multi-Factor Risk Formula (0–100)

50% WEIGHT Hazard & Physical Danger
  • Physical defects: deadwood, root decay, trunk split, severe lean
  • Target impact: electrical powerlines, road clearance, schools
  • Evidence synthesis: visual proof supersedes written keywords
25% WEIGHT Wait Time Escalation
  • Linear escalation curve: 0 to 180 days (capped at 100 pts)
  • Guarantees aging complaints are never permanently starved
  • Calibrated so low-risk calls never leapfrog immediate hazards
15% WEIGHT Location & Public Impact
  • Arterials (Spring Garden, Robie) scored 75–100 pts
  • Residential streets scored 40–50 pts; quiet parks 20–30 pts
  • Evaluates pedestrian, vehicular, and transit corridor density
10% WEIGHT Data Confidence & Review
  • Vague text with missing photos flagged with 100 pt review score
  • High-confidence verified photos receive 0 pt penalty
  • Forces field verification when incoming data is incomplete
